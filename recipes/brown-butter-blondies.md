title: Brown Butter Blondies
category: baking
time: 50 min
serves: 12
blurb: Nutty, chewy-edged, a little salty on top. Keeps well for four days, if it lasts that long.
tags: [dessert, make-ahead]
ingredients: |
  1 cup butter, browned
  1½ cups brown sugar
  2 eggs, plus 1 yolk
  1¾ cups flour
  1 tsp baking powder
  Flaky salt
permalink: "recipes/{{ title | slug }}/"
---
1. Brown the butter until nutty and amber, then cool slightly.
2. Whisk into sugar, then eggs.
3. Fold in dry ingredients — do not overmix.
4. Bake at 350°F, 28–32 minutes. Salt the top while warm.
