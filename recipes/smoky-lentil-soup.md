---
layout: recipe.njk
title: Smoky Lentil Soup
category: soups
time: 45 min
serves: 6
blurb: Pantry lentils, smoked paprika, a finish of good olive oil and lemon.
tags: [vegetarian, freezer-friendly, pantry]
ingredients: |
  1½ cups brown lentils
  1 onion, diced
  2 carrots, diced
  2 tsp smoked paprika
  6 cups stock
  Olive oil and lemon, to finish
permalink: "recipes/{{ title | slug }}/"
---
1. Sweat onion and carrot until soft, about 8 minutes.
2. Add paprika, toast 30 seconds.
3. Add lentils and stock, simmer 30 minutes until tender.
4. Finish each bowl with a drizzle of oil and a squeeze of lemon.
